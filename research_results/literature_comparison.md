# Related Work and Comparative Analysis

## Progent (2025)

Progent implements programmable privilege control for
LLM agents through fine-grained tool-call policies.

## CaMeL (2025)

CaMeL separates trusted control flow from untrusted
data and uses capability-based security enforcement
to defend against prompt injection.

## AgentShield

AgentShield uses human-defined declarative policies,
task-scoped authorization, default-deny enforcement,
human approval and audit logging.

## Research Contribution

AgentShield investigates the security and utility
trade-offs of explicit human-defined authorization
policies in autonomous AI agents.

External policy enforcement is not itself novel.
The contribution must be established through
comparative experimental evidence.

## Limitations

Direct experimental comparisons against Progent
and CaMeL have not been performed. This comparison
is based on their published research.
